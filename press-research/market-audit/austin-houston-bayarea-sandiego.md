# Market audit: Austin, Houston, SF Bay Area, San Diego County

Audit date: 2026-09-29. All URLs below were fetched on that date unless noted.
Source labels: **[club site]** = verified on the club's own website or its own booking page (Playtomic / PlayByPoint / OpenCourt club page); **[secondary]** = verified on a news outlet, federation (USA Padel) or other third party; **[not found]** = looked for, could not verify.

Known limits of this pass:
- Several club sites block automated fetches (parkpadel.com, book.west43padel.com, sunnyvale.baypadel.us, dogpatch.baypadel.us, book.padeln9ne.com returned 403 / bot challenge). Where that happened the club's Playtomic page or main-domain page was used instead.
- Instagram/Facebook were not read. "Open" status for very recent openings relies on the sources cited.
- CourtSource city guides list venues we could not verify anywhere else (wrong addresses, "OLD" duplicates, flat "4 courts" defaults). None of those were counted. They are listed per market under "Not counted".

# MARKET 1 — Austin TX

## Why Axios/Playtomic said 42 and we said 23

Axios Austin (2026-06-30) quoted Playtomic: Austin 42 courts, #2 nationally behind Miami (192), ahead of Houston (41). Axios named Padel 39's two locations, Padel Club Austin, Padel Society ATX, Austin Padel Center and Dripping Springs Racquet Club.
- Source: https://www.axios.com/local/austin/2026/06/30/padel-in-austin-fast-growing-racquet-sport (article itself returns 403 to fetch; figures read from search-result extract) **[secondary]**

The Playtomic club pages today add up like this (this reconstruction is my inference, not a Playtomic statement):

| Club on Playtomic | Bookable padel courts |
|---|---|
| Padel Club Austin | 9 |
| Padel39 North Austin | 9 |
| Padel39 East Austin | 12 |
| Legacy Padel & Pickleball Austin | 9 |
| Austin Padel Center (3 indoor courts open March to early August) | 3 |
| **Total** | **42** |

So the gap is: (a) Padel39 East Austin is open, we had it as announced (12 courts); (b) Legacy Padel & Pickleball is missing from our directory entirely (9 courts); (c) Padel39 North shows 9 courts on Playtomic vs our 6; (d) Austin Padel Center has since opened its permanent 9-court building. Dripping Springs (3 courts) is not on Playtomic, so the true number is higher than Playtomic's.
